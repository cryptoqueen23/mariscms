## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- Lemon
- Fresh herbs
- Seasonal vegetables

## Method

Season the chicken generously. Roast until the thickest part reaches a safe internal temperature. Rest before carving and serve with the vegetables.

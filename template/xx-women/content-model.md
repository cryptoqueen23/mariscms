# XX WOMEN Content Model

## Features

- title
- dek
- slug
- department
- author
- publishDate
- updatedDate
- heroImage
- heroAlt
- imageCredit
- tags
- featured
- package
- issue
- contentType
- seoTitle
- seoDescription
- sources
- disclosure
- body

## Departments

Style, Beauty, Home, Food, Wellness, Love & Family, Money, Work, Travel, Culture, Faith, Pro-Life, Politics, Voices, Shop.

## Content types

- Feature
- Report
- Profile
- Interview
- Essay
- Guide
- Review
- Recipe
- How-To
- Column
- Shopping
- News Brief

## Issues

Each issue can contain:

- coverTitle
- coverImage
- theme
- issueDate
- issueNumber
- featuredStories
- departments
- contributors
- editorLetter

## Reviews

Review content should support:

- product
- category
- price
- testedBy
- verdict
- pros
- cons
- disclosure

## Voices

Voices are commentary and personal essays. They must be visibly distinguished from reported journalism.

## Politics

Politics supports factual reporting and explainers. Opinion is a separate content type and should never be disguised as news.

## Commerce

Shop content can include product links, affiliate disclosures and advertiser relationships without changing the editorial template.
